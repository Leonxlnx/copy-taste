How it works

Slow at the start. Fast at the end.

The expensive mistakes in branding happen in week one, when nobody has asked the awkward questions yet. So we ask them early, and loudly.

Weeks 1–3

Dig

We interview your customers, your sales team and your harshest critics. You get a written findings report before we draw a single shape.

Weeks 4–6

Shape

Two strategic routes, each with its own story, rough visuals and a sample headline. You pick one, and we argue for the one we'd choose.

Weeks 7–12

Fire

The chosen route gets the full treatment: identity, packaging, website, voice. We test it on real shelves, real screens and real people.

Weeks 13–16

Hand over

Guidelines, templates and a half-day workshop for your team. Then we check in at three, six and twelve months, at no extra cost.

The studio

A former tile factory, still making things that last.

We named the studio after the old kiln on our ground floor, which fired roof tiles for half of Rotterdam until 1971. It's a reminder that the good stuff takes heat, patience and a little nerve.

We stay deliberately small. Every project is led by a founder, and the people who pitch your work are the people who make it.
