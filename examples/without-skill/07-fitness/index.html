The studio

A greenhouse full of people growing at their own pace.

When we found the old Alder Street greenhouse in 2019, it had broken panes, a tangle of wild ferns and the best morning light in the neighborhood. We kept the glass, kept the ferns, and built a warm wooden floor underneath them.

Today it is home to a community of beginners, runners, new parents, retirees and a few people who swore they would never do yoga. Nobody here is performing. We are just practicing.

Small rooms

Fourteen mats per class, so every person gets real coaching.

No mirrors

We teach you to feel a pose, not to watch yourself in it.

Every body

Every class offers options for stiff hips, sore wrists and bad days.

Classes

Six ways to practice, one honest goal.

Whether you want to sweat, stretch or simply sleep better, there is a class that fits your week. Not sure where to begin? Slow Flow and Foundations Strength are built for first-timers.

60 minAll levels

Sunrise Vinyasa

A steady, breath-led flow that builds heat and finishes in a long, quiet rest. The way we like to start a day.

60 minBeginner

Slow Flow

The same shapes as vinyasa at half the speed, with time to learn the alignment that keeps your joints happy.

50 minAll levels

Foundations Strength

Kettlebells, dumbbells and bodyweight work in small groups. We coach squats, hinges and presses until they feel natural.

45 minIntermediate

Mat Pilates

Precise, controlled core work with rings and light bands. Expect to shake a little and stand taller afterward.

75 minAll levels

Candlelit Yin

Long, supported holds on bolsters and blankets under low light. The evening class our regulars guard most closely.

60 minPre & postnatal

Bloom Prenatal

Gentle strength, pelvic floor work and breathing for every trimester, taught by a certified prenatal specialist.
