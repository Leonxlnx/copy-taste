Why we started

Your skin doesn’t need more. It needs less, done well.

Ebb & Kelp began in 2021 at a kitchen table in Belfast, Maine, when our founder Nora Hale gave up on the forty-product shelf that was making her sensitive skin worse. She went back to what her grandmother used after long days on the water: seaweed, oats, and a little patience.

Cold-water kelp grows slowly in nutrient-rich water, and it is packed with minerals, polysaccharides, and natural antioxidants that help skin hold moisture and settle down. We build every formula around it, then stop adding things the moment the job is done.

The ritual

Four steps. About three minutes.

Morning and night, in this order. If you only have time for two, cleanse and serum.

Wash

Morning & night

Massage a coin-sized amount of Tidewash onto dry skin for 30 seconds, then rinse with lukewarm water.

Mist

Morning & night

Two or three sprays of Sea Fennel Mist while skin is still damp. It helps everything after it absorb better.

Treat

Morning & night

Press three drops of Low Tide Serum into your face and neck. Follow with sunscreen in the morning.

Seal

Night only

Warm a pea-sized scoop of Driftwood Balm between your fingertips and press it on as the last step before bed.

Our harvest

We know the people who cut our seaweed by name.

Our kelp, bladderwrack, and sea moss come from three family harvesters working the ledges of Penobscot Bay. They cut by hand at low tide, leaving the holdfast so every plant grows back by the next season.

The seaweed is rinsed in seawater, air-dried in the sun, and delivered to our workshop in Belfast within a week. We mix in batches of 400 units so nothing sits on a warehouse shelf for months.

Good questions

Will it work for sensitive or acne-prone skin?

That’s who we formulate for first. Every product is fragrance-free, dermatologist-tested, and non-comedogenic. If you have a known seaweed or iodine sensitivity, check with your doctor before trying.

Does it smell like the ocean?

Barely. You might catch a faint, clean mineral note when you open the bottle, but nothing lingers on your skin.

How long does each product last?

Used twice daily, Tidewash and the Mist last about 10 weeks, Low Tide Serum about 8 weeks, and Driftwood Balm about 12 weeks of nightly use.

What if it doesn’t work for me?

Send it back within 60 days, even if the bottle is empty, and we’ll refund you in full. No forms, no back-and-forth.
