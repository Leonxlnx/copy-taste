The studio

A small practice that stays with a building until it is lived in.

Isla Aldgrave and Tomas Reyne founded the studio in a former cooperage in Leith with one conviction: a good building begins with a long look at the place it will stand.

Fifteen years on, we are still a single team in a single room. Every project is led by a partner from first sketch to final snag, and the people who draw your building are the people who stand on site with your contractor.

We favour materials that weather well and can be repaired by local trades. We design for low running energy, model daylight before we fix a window, and treat a tight budget as a brief, not an obstacle.

Selected work

Four recent buildings, four very different sites.

A sample from the last three years. Full case studies, drawings and cost summaries are available on request for any project.

Saltings House

Aberlady, East Lothian

Private house
2024

A three-bedroom home behind the dunes, clad in larch that will silver within five winters. Passivhaus-certified, heated by a single air-source pump.

Kirkgate Library

Linlithgow, West Lothian

Civic, reuse
2023

An 1890s drill hall turned town library. We kept the stone shell, opened the arcade to the street and added a reading room under the old trusses.

Braid Hill Workshops

Edinburgh

Workplace
2025

Twelve makers' studios for a furniture cooperative, under north-facing sawtooth roofs that give even light all day. Built in cross-laminated timber in nine weeks.

Tarbet Boathouse

Loch Lomond, Argyll

Restoration
2024

A collapsed Victorian boathouse rebuilt on its original footings as a rowing club and small guest room. The stone came from the ruin; the slate came from the next glen.

How a project runs

Clear stages, honest timelines.

Most houses take eighteen months to two years from first meeting to moving in. You will know the fee, the programme and the next decision at every stage.

  1. 1

    Walk the site

    We spend a day on your land or in your building, with you. We measure the light, the wind and the views, and we listen to how you want to live or work.

    Weeks 1–3
  2. 2

    Sketch and test

    Three distinct options, each with card models and a cost range from our quantity surveyor. You choose one direction and we refine it together.

    Weeks 4–12
  3. 3

    Secure consent

    We prepare and submit planning and building warrant drawings, and meet the planners in person. Most of our applications are approved first time.

    Months 3–8
  4. 4

    Build it well

    We tender to contractors we trust, then visit site every week. The partner who drew your first sketch signs off the finished building.

    Months 8–22
