Our story

A former hardware store, a secondhand roaster, and a lot of early mornings.

Crowfoot started in 2019 when Mara and Theo Lindqvist signed a lease on the old Wren Street Hardware building with no furniture and one very heavy roaster they bought from a retiring roaster upstate. They spent six months rebuilding it, and named it Gus after the shop's original owner.

We still roast every batch on Gus, still pay our farmers well above the commodity price, and still keep the menu short enough to fit on one chalkboard. You'll find the original hardware drawers behind the counter. They hold spoons now.

How we work

From green bean to your cup in about nine days.

Freshness isn't a slogan here. It's the schedule we've kept every week since we opened.

01
Every season

We buy direct

We work with five farms we've visited in person, and we pay at least double the commodity price for every bag we buy.

02
Tuesday

We roast

Gus runs from 4am to noon. Each batch is logged, cupped and tasted before anything leaves the building.

03
Wednesday to Friday

We let it rest

Freshly roasted coffee needs a few days to settle. We dial in every coffee only after it's ready, not before.

04
The weekend after

We pour

It goes into the grinder, onto the shelf and out the door. Anything older than a month goes into cold brew.
