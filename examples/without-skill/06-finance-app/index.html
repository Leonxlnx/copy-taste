How it works

Three steps, then it mostly runs itself.

You don't have to categorize every coffee. Keel learns your patterns and does the tedious parts, so you only check in when something actually needs you.

Connect your accounts

Link checking, savings and cards in a few taps. Keel reads your history and spots your paycheck, rent and recurring bills on its own.

Tell it what matters

Add the goals you care about, like a trip, a cushion or paying off a card. Keel works out how much to set aside each payday to get there on time.

The safe-to-spend number

One number you can trust, because you can see the math.

Most apps show you a balance and leave you to guess. Keel starts with what you have, subtracts everything already spoken for, and spreads the rest across the days until your next paycheck.

It updates as you go. Buy lunch and the number drops right away. Get a refund and it comes back.
It forgives bad days. Overspend on Tuesday and Keel spreads the difference across the rest of the week instead of flashing red.
It handles odd paychecks. Freelance, tips or shifts that change week to week. Keel plans around your low months, not your best ones.

Security and privacy

Your money stays where it is. Your data stays yours.

Keel can see your transactions. It can never move money, and it will never sell what it sees. That's not a setting. It's how we built it.

Read-only by design

We connect through regulated data partners with view-only permissions. Nobody at Keel can transfer a cent.

No ads, no data sales

You pay us, so we don't need to profit from your spending habits. We don't sell, rent or share your data with advertisers.

Bank-grade encryption

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Your bank login details never touch our servers.

Leave whenever you want

Export everything to a spreadsheet anytime. Delete your account and your data is gone within 30 days.
