03 / Process

No black box. No big reveal.

You see the work every week, while it's still cheap to change. Most projects follow this rhythm, stretched or squeezed to fit.

Week 1

Listen

I talk to your team, your customers and your data until I can explain the problem better than the brief does.

You get: a one-page problem statement
Week 2

Frame

We agree on what success looks like and which ideas are worth testing. Then we cut the rest, kindly but firmly.

You get: success metrics and flow maps
Weeks 3 – 8

Make

Prototypes, real users, revisions. Engineers review early so nothing I hand over is a surprise or a fantasy.

You get: tested, dev-ready designs
Launch +

Ship

I stay through build and release, answering questions and checking the details that tend to slip in the last week.

You get: a launch review at 30 days

04 / About

Hi, I'm Saskia.

I like the unglamorous parts of software: the settings page, the error message, the moment someone comes back after three weeks away and needs to remember where they were.

I studied interaction design in Malmö, then spent six years in-house, most recently as lead designer at Møller Health, where I helped rebuild a patient portal used by 1.2 million people. In 2021 I went independent so I could work across more problems with fewer meetings.

Today I take on four or five projects a year, usually with teams of five to fifty. I write as much as I draw, I'm happiest in a shared Figma file with an engineer, and I'll tell you early if I think the thing we're building shouldn't be built.
